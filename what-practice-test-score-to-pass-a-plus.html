What practice score do you need to pass A+?

Last updated 2026-10-18

There's no official number, but in the pass posts we collected, people scoring roughly 70-85% on Dion's practice exams passed, and one person passed both cores with 63% and 75% on CertMaster Practice. A common rule of thumb on r/CompTIA is about 75-80% on Dion. Treat it as a guide, not a guarantee: these are 9 self-reported data points.

FAQ

Is 75% on Dion enough to pass A+?

Several passers in our data scored in the 70s on Dion and passed (for example 77-83% before an 823). It's not guaranteed.

Why are my CertMaster scores so low?

Passers report low CertMaster scores too. One passed both cores after 63% and 75%.

Can I convert a practice % to the 900 scale?

No. CompTIA doesn't publish its scoring weights.
