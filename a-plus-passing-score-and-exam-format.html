A+ passing score and exam format

Last updated 2026-10-18

You need 675 to pass 220-1201 (Core 1) and 700 to pass 220-1202 (Core 2), on a scale of 100-900. Each exam has up to 90 questions in 90 minutes: multiple-choice (single and multiple response), drag-and-drop and performance-based questions. You need both exams, from the same version (V15), to earn A+.

FAQ

What is the A+ 1201 passing score?

675 on a 100-900 scale.

What is the A+ 1202 passing score?

700 on a 100-900 scale.

Is 675 out of 900 75%?

Not exactly. The score is scaled, and CompTIA doesn't publish how questions are weighted.
