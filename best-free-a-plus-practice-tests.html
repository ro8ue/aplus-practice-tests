Best free A+ practice tests (220-1201/1202)

Last updated 2026-10-18

The best free options we found: ExamCompass (free, no registration, 22 Core 1 practice tests plus a Core 2 section), Crucial Exams' free tier (up to 20 questions per test from a 1,000-question 220-1201 bank), and BurningIceTech's YouTube videos (170 questions per core, with explanations). For a real PBQ interface, CompTIA's own example simulation at demosim.comptia.org is free.

FAQ

Is there a free official CompTIA A+ practice test?

CompTIA offers a free example simulation (one PBQ) at demosim.comptia.org. We didn't find a free official full practice exam.

Are ExamCompass tests up to date?

ExamCompass lists separate 220-1201 and 220-1202 sections based on current domains.

How many free questions does Crucial Exams give?

Free users are limited to 20 questions per practice test, per their site.
