Methodology & disclosure

  • Facts (price, question count, PBQs, coverage) come from each product's own page or listing. We record the check date and mark anything we couldn't confirm.
  • Difficulty summaries come only from public Reddit posts by test takers. We quote briefly and link every source. Opinions vary, and we show the dissenting ones too.
  • Practice vs real scores are self-reported data points from public pass posts, each linked. We don't invent or estimate numbers.
  • "Best for" picks are our editorial judgment from the above. They aren't paid.
  • Facts last checked: 2026-10-18. We plan to re-check weekly.
