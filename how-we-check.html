How we check prices

  1. At least once a week we open every seller's public A+ voucher page and record the price shown, the voucher type, the expiry, retake terms and region limits, plus the date we checked.
  2. We only list prices we saw ourselves on that date. If a page won't load or the price isn't clear, we leave the seller out and say so.
  3. Anything a seller doesn't state is marked "not listed".
  4. Prices can change at any time. The seller's checkout price is the real one.
