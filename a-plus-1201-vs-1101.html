A+ 220-1201 vs 220-1101: what changed

Last updated 2026-10-18

220-1201 (V15, launched March 25, 2025) replaces 220-1101 for Core 1. The domains are the same, but the weights shifted: Networking went up from 20% to 23%, Mobile Devices went down from 15% to 13%, and Troubleshooting went from 29% to 28%. Professor Messer estimates about 87% of the topics are unchanged. New topics include 6 GHz Wi-Fi, SIM/eSIM, MDM policies, containers and hypervisor types. You can't mix versions: Core 1 and Core 2 must both be V15.

Does 1101 material still work?

Mostly, but not entirely. One passer used 1101/1102 flashcards for the new exams and estimated about 80% overlap (r/CompTIA). Messer recommends studying from materials that match your exam version. Prowse notes the 6-step troubleshooting process is no longer an objective.

FAQ

Can I take 1101 and 1202?

No. CompTIA says Core 1 and Core 2 must be from the same version.

Is the passing score different?

V15 is 675 (Core 1) and 700 (Core 2) on a 100-900 scale, per CompTIA.

When does V15 retire?

CompTIA estimates 2028 (usually three years after launch).
