Where to practise A+ PBQs

Last updated 2026-10-18

Start with CompTIA's free example simulation (demosim.comptia.org) to see the real interface. For more volume: Crucial Exams has 12 PBQs for 220-1201 (full access with Premium), Messer's practice exams include performance-based questions, CertMaster Practice includes PBQs, Dion sells a separate 50-question PBQ pack for Core 1 ($29.99), and BurningIceTech offers PBQs on Patreon. Passers consistently say the real PBQs were the hardest part of the exam.

FAQ

How many PBQs are on the A+ exam?

CompTIA says each exam has up to 90 questions, including performance-based ones, but doesn't publish a fixed PBQ count.

Are there free A+ PBQs?

CompTIA's demosim.comptia.org has one free example. Crucial Exams previews PBQ formats for free.

Should I skip PBQs and come back?

It's a common tip in pass posts, but it's not official guidance.
