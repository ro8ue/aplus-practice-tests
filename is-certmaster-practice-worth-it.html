Is CertMaster Practice worth it for A+?

Last updated 2026-10-18

It's worth it if you want practice in CompTIA's own question style and can afford it. If not, cheaper banks cover the same objectives. CertMaster Practice is CompTIA's official tool: timed practice tests, objective quizzes and mastery scores, with 12 months of access. We haven't confirmed its price on CompTIA's own store; one third-party listing shows $149 regular and $124 on sale for Core 1. One passer called it "AMAZING for retention and practice with CompTIAs question format" but never passed a single practice test (63% and 75%) and still passed both exams.

What it includes

Per CompTIA: timed and untimed practice tests, exam objective assessments, and mastery score reporting. CompTIA positions it as "best for those having experience", with an estimated 10-20 hours of use (A+ page).

Who should skip it

If you're on a tight budget, Crucial Exams' free tier, ExamCompass and BurningIceTech are free, and Dion and Messer cost far less. Don't panic over low CertMaster scores: the data point above passed with 63% and 75%.

FAQ

How much is CertMaster Practice for A+?

We couldn't confirm it on CompTIA's store. A third-party listing shows $149 regular and $124 sale for Core 1. Check CompTIA's A+ page.

Does CertMaster Practice have PBQs?

A seller listing says it includes a timed practice test with performance-based questions.

Is CertMaster Practice the same as CertMaster Learn?

No. Learn is a full course (25-40 hours) with labs. Practice is for readiness checks (10-20 hours), per CompTIA.
